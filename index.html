About me

Bridging the gap between psychology, sociology & tech.

I'm Rinny, a Public Relations graduate. I started my career in corporate, led an international blockchain conference at 23, built influencer campaigns at one of Malaysia's biggest media agencies, then founded a social impact enterprise to make mental health recovery creative and community-driven.

Today I work as an advocate and educator, combining instructional design, community building and creative facilitation to build the literacy that matters most at work: emotional, digital and social.
